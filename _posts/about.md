My name is <i>Angelica Bonilla Fominaya</i> and I am a Pittsburgh-based Colombian fine artist, roboticist and doctoral student at Carnegie Mellon's Robotics Institute working in the Textiles Lab, co-advised by Dr. James McCann and Dr. Andrew Spielberg. I also go by <b> A. B. Fominaya</b>, primarily in my creative practice. 
<br>
<br>
In both my art and research, I am interested in exploring different ways in which people can collaborate with machines, specifically towards co-creation. My recent work explores computerized machine knitting, plotters, and creative interactions with collaborative robot arms to address different elements of generativity and machine interaction.
<br>
<br>
My work has been featured in the JMEM 2023 Exhibit, Machine Arts Magazine, and other publications. I currently have a mural permally on display at Carnegie Mellon's Posner Hall.
