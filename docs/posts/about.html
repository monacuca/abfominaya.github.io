About Me

Angelica Bonilla (A.B.) Fominaya
May, 2020
Tags: about

My name is Angelica Bonilla Fominaya and I am a Pittsburgh-based Colombian fine artist, roboticist and doctoral student at Carnegie Mellon's Robotics Institute working in the Textiles Lab, co-advised by Dr. James McCann and Dr. Andrew Spielberg. I also go by A. B. Fominaya, primarily in my creative practice. In both my art and research, I am interested in exploring different ways in which people can collaborate with machines, specifically towards co-creation. My recent work explores computerized machine knitting, plotters, and creative interactions with collaborative robot arms to address different elements of generativity and machine interaction. My work has been featured in the JMEM 2023 Exhibit, Machine Arts Magazine, and other publications. I currently have a mural permally on display at Carnegie Mellon's Posner Hall.
